GyroCare Pro

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Summary

GyroCare Pro stores your data on your device. There are no user accounts, no cloud sync, and no server operated by us. We do not collect, receive, store, or sell any personal information.

The only data that leaves your device is your approximate location, sent to third-party weather and mapping services so the app can show you wind, density altitude and place names. You can turn this off.

1. Who we are

GyroCare Pro ("the app") is published by Ahmar Shaikh.

Contact: ahmarautogyro@gmail.com Address: Waco, TX 76705, United States

2. Data we collect

We collect nothing. The developer operates no server and receives no data from the app. We have no ability to see your maintenance records, flight logbook, aircraft details, or location.

3. Data stored on your device

The app stores the following locally on your device only:

  • Aircraft identity and configuration (registration, type, model, engine, rotor system, owner name, address, colour)
  • Maintenance schedule and completion history
  • Flight logbook entries and GPS flight tracks
  • Your last known location and any manually chosen location
  • App preferences (display mode, aircraft options, diagnostics setting)

This data is never transmitted to us or to any third party. It remains on the device until you delete it or delete the app.

4. Location

Purpose. The app uses your device location to retrieve local weather, wind and density altitude, and to record GPS flight tracks if you choose to use flight tracking.

What is shared. When fetching weather, your geographic coordinates are sent to the third-party services listed in section 5. Nothing else is sent with them — no identifier, no aircraft data, no records.

Your control. Location is optional. You may:

  • Decline the location permission when the app requests it
  • Set a location manually in the Weather tab instead
  • Revoke location access at any time in iOS Settings

Declining location disables weather features and GPS flight tracking. All maintenance and logbook features continue to work.

Retention. Your most recent coordinates are cached on your device so the app can show data on launch. They are not transmitted to us.

5. Third-party services

The app contacts the following services. Each receives only the coordinates needed to answer the request.

Service Purpose Privacy policy
Open-Meteo Weather and forecast data https://open-meteo.com/en/terms
CheckWX Aviation weather (METAR) https://www.checkwxapi.com/privacy
Mapbox Maps and place-name lookup https://www.mapbox.com/legal/privacy

These are independent companies with their own privacy practices. We do not control what they do with requests they receive, and we receive nothing back about you. Review their policies if this matters to you.

6. What the app does NOT do

  • No user accounts, sign-in, or passwords
  • No cloud storage or sync
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising or advertising identifiers
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No sale or sharing of personal information

Subscriptions are billed and managed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or store your payment details.

7. Data you export

The app lets you export records, save backup files, and produce PDFs. Anything you export leaves the app's control and becomes your responsibility. If you share an export, be aware it may contain your aircraft registration, owner name and address.

8. Children

The app is intended for pilots and aircraft owners and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

9. Security

Your data is protected by your device's own security — passcode, Face ID or Touch ID, and iOS file encryption. Because nothing is transmitted to us, there is no server-side breach risk for your records. Keep your device secured and your backups somewhere safe.

10. Your rights

Under GDPR, CCPA and similar laws you have rights to access, correct, delete and port your personal data. Because all data stays on your device:

  • Access — open the app; it is all there
  • Correct — edit it in the app
  • Delete — delete records in the app, or delete the app to remove everything
  • Port — use the export and backup functions in Setup

We hold nothing about you, so there is nothing for us to retrieve or delete on your behalf. No request to us is necessary.
