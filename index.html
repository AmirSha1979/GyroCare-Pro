Under the cowling

Serious about maintenance

GyroCare Pro is built around the official AutoGyro documentation — not generic checklists.

1

Every task from the manuals

Complete maintenance schedules from the official AutoGyro manuals, for every model — matched to your exact aircraft and engine.

2

Service bulletins, automatically

The app checks the AutoGyro website for new service bulletins and surfaces them under the affected tasks.

3

Deep-links to the manual

Each task links to the exact manual section with the procedure details and pictures.

4

Tach-driven scheduling

100-hour, annual and calendar items count down against your tachometer hours — with overdue and due-soon warnings.

5

Your data stays yours

Everything lives on your device. No accounts, no cloud, no tracking. Export backups and PDFs whenever you like.

6

Fly, then relive it

Track flights with GPS and export any of them to Google Earth (KML) straight from the logbook.
