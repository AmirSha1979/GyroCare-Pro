The story

"I couldn't find an app that treated my gyroplane like a real aircraft. So I built one."

GyroCare Pro was designed and built by a gyroplane pilot in Waco, Texas — and flown, tested and refined on his own AutoGyro, flight after flight.

Every schedule comes from the official AutoGyro manuals. Every feature exists because a pilot needed it on the ramp, not because it looked good in a boardroom.

Built by a pilot, for pilots. That's not a slogan — it's the development process.

Questions

Before you ask

Which aircraft does it support?

All AutoGyro models are included, with each model's own maintenance schedule matched to your exact engine and rotor combination.

Where does my data live?

On your iPhone — only. No accounts, no cloud, no tracking. You can export backups, CSVs and PDFs whenever you like.

How does the free month work?

Download the app and you get 30 days of full access from first launch — no credit card, no trial to cancel. After that, subscribe monthly or yearly to keep going.

Does it work without signal at the airfield?

Yes. Your schedules, logbook and history live on the device. Weather and bulletin checks update whenever you have a connection.

Is there an Android version?

GyroCare Pro is iPhone-only for now. An Android version will follow if enough pilots ask for one — tell us at support if that's you.

Who do I contact for help?

Email ahmarautogyro@gmail.com — you'll hear back from the pilot who built it.
